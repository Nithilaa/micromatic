01 / About Us

Three decades on the shop floor,
machining to a tolerance you can trust.

Athipalayam, Coimbatore

Established in 1994, Micro Matic is a distinguished name in the manufacturing and supply of precision machined components. Our production facility in Coimbatore is equipped to meet the exacting demands of a wide range of industries.

We specialize in CNC Milling, Boring, Turning and Cylindrical Grinding, producing textile machinery components, machine tool components, automobile components, pumps & valve components, hydraulic manifold blocks and machined castings. Supported by robust procurement processes with a variety of suppliers, we're equipped to meet customer requirements at scale.

Our capabilities extend beyond machined components to include assemblies and sub-assemblies, enhanced by specialized facilities for heat treatment, painting, coating and more.

CNC Milling Boring Turning Cylindrical Grinding Assemblies Heat Treatment Painting & Coating
Mission

To deliver high-quality precision machined components and assemblies that meet international standards — on time and at a reasonable cost. We're driven by operational excellence, customer satisfaction and continuous improvement, supported by robust processes and skilled professionals. Our zero-defect policy underpins our dedication to quality at every stage of production.

Vision

To be a respected leader in precision machining, known for our ability to consistently provide reliable, cost-effective and timely solutions. Through modern technology, a talented workforce and a focus on quality and efficiency, we build long-term value for our customers — integrating innovation and discipline with a strong foundation in zero-defect principles.

02 / Manufacturing Facility

A precision floor,
centrally placed for South India.

Located in the industrial city of Coimbatore, centrally positioned to Bangalore, Chennai & Kochi. The facility spans roughly 15,000 sq. ft, operating since 1994 with a professional workforce and well-equipped machines — enabling us to manufacture to international standards, efficiently.

Milling

4-axis VMC machining across LMW JV Kraft, JV130, JV100, J2 & LV-series centres.

Turning

LMW Pilatus, L10, Pinacho & HMT Econo turning centres up to 2000mm length.

Grinding

HMT cylindrical grinding on G17 & G22 machines for fine surface finishing.

Finishing

In-house heat treatment, painting, coating and roller balancing up to 300kg.

Stage 01 / 04

From raw casting to a part that fits.

  1. 01

    Fixture and clamp

    Every job gets its own fixture, so the part sits exactly where the program expects it.

  2. 02

    Mill on 4 axes

    Faces, pockets, bores and hole patterns are cut on our LMW VMCs with 4th-axis rotaries.

  3. 03

    Turn to size

    Shafts, rollers and rings come off the turning centres up to 2000 mm long.

  4. 04

    Grind, finish, inspect

    Cylindrical grinding for the final surface, then measured against our zero-defect policy.
